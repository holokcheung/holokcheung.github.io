Hackathon · Cal Hacks 13.0 · 2026

Swift & RoomPlanLiDARReactFastAPIClaudeDot Pad

Tactile and audio maps for blind and visually impaired people

Scan a room with a LiDAR iPhone and TouchSpace turns it into a floor plan of walls, doors, and landmarks that rises as pins on a Dot Pad tactile display. Ask questions out loud and Claude answers from the real map data. Every route is computed straight from the map, so the AI can never make up a path. Built with James Zheng and Sunghyuk Yoon.

🏆Claude for Public Benefit Prize · $5K from Anthropic

An AI pronunciation trainer for English learners
